1. FEATURE 1: PEAK CUSTOMER SHOPPING HOURS
================================================================================
WHAT IT IS:
A simple 24-hour bar chart or colored clock showing which hours of the day
local shoppers are viewing the store or browsing items.

WHY SHOPKEEPERS CARE:
- They learn when customer footfall and online demand are highest.
- Example: If traffic spikes between 6:00 PM and 9:00 PM, the merchant
  knows to launch Flash Deals at 5:30 PM.

DATA TRACKED:
- Hour timestamp (0 to 23) recorded whenever an analytics event occurs.
- Grouped into 4 clean time blocks on mobile:
  * Morning (6 AM - 12 PM)
  * Afternoon (12 PM - 5 PM)
  * Evening (5 PM - 9 PM) [Peak Badge]
  * Night (9 PM - 6 AM)

================================================================================
2. FEATURE 2: TOP SEARCH KEYWORDS
================================================================================
WHAT IT IS:
A list of the top 5 to 10 search queries typed by local buyers that brought
them to this merchant's shop or products.

WHY SHOPKEEPERS CARE:
- Tells the seller exactly what customers in their town are looking for.
- Example: If "Party Kurti" has 50 searches but the seller only listed
  "Simple Kurti", the seller can add more party wear to make more sales.

DATA TRACKED:
- Search query string from BuyerDiscover search bar.
- Matched store or product ID.
- Search count tally per store over 7, 28, or 90 days.

================================================================================
3. FEATURE 3: COMPLETE CUSTOMER ACTIONS FUNNEL
================================================================================
WHAT IT IS:
A visual step-by-step funnel card showing every high-intent action buyers take:
- Step 1: Store & Product Impressions (How many saw the card)
- Step 2: Product Detail Opens (How many clicked to read more)
- Step 3: Direct WhatsApp Inquiries (Clicked green WhatsApp chat)
- Step 4: Phone Call Clicks (Clicked Call Store button)
- Step 5: Get Directions / Map Opens (Clicked to navigate to physical shop)
- Step 6: Flash Deal Claimed (Clicked to claim time-limited discount)

WHY SHOPKEEPERS CARE:
- Proves real value: Even if a buyer does not message on WhatsApp, seeing
  "12 people opened directions to your shop" proves LocalFind is bringing
  real foot traffic into their physical store.

================================================================================
4. FEATURE 4: AI SMART BUSINESS ADVISOR (POWERED BY GEMINI)
================================================================================
WHAT IT IS:
A one-click button: "Generate AI Business Advice".
Google Gemini reads the merchant's last 7 days of numbers (views, clicks, top
search words, zero-click products) and gives 2 short, bullet-point tips.

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:
* Tip 1: "Your 'Wireless Earbuds' got 60 views but 0 WhatsApp inquiries.
  Try reducing the price by 5% or adding a 24-hour Flash Deal."
* Tip 2: "Evening traffic between 7 PM - 9 PM is up by 40%. Make sure your
  shop status is set to 'Open' during these hours."

WHY SHOPKEEPERS CARE:
- Saves them from guessing what the numbers mean.
- Gives them a personal digital business consultant right inside their phone.

================================================================================
5. FEATURE 5: NEARBY CUSTOMER AREA / PINCODE REACH
================================================================================
WHAT IT IS:
A clean list of top nearby areas and postal pincodes where the shoppers were
located when browsing this store.

WHY SHOPKEEPERS CARE:
- Shows the merchant which neighborhoods love their store the most.
- Helps them decide where to distribute physical pamphlets or focus marketing.

7. DATABASE & BACKEND CHANGES NEEDED
================================================================================
1. Update `analytics_events` table in Cloudflare D1:
   - Add `hour_of_day INTEGER` (0-23)
   - Add `search_query TEXT` (optional, for search click events)
   - Add `buyer_pincode TEXT` (optional, from user location header/cookie)
   - Add `action_type TEXT` ('view', 'whatsapp', 'call', 'directions', 'flash')

2. New API endpoints in Cloudflare Worker (`worker/src/index.js`):
   - `GET /api/analytics/advanced?shop_id=...&days=...`
     Returns:
     * `hourly_distribution`: Array of 24 hours with counts
     * `top_keywords`: Array of [{ query: "kurti", count: 18 }, ...]
     * `funnel`: { views: 120, details: 65, whatsapp: 14, calls: 8, directions: 19 }
     * `area_reach`: [{ pincode: "110001", area: "Connaught Place", count: 42 }, ...]
   - `POST /api/analytics/ai-insights`
     Sends summary metrics to Gemini Flash and returns 2 actionable tips.
